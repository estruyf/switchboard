## LinkedIn

Switchboard now has a VS Code companion.

Switchboard is my macOS app for running and managing Claude Code sessions. Until now, getting context from your editor into a session meant copying paths or dragging files across. The new Switchboard Companion extension does that for you.

What it does:

• Select lines, right-click, and choose "Add Selection to Switchboard". They arrive as a chip in the session's message box.
• Send the open file, files and folders from the Explorer, open editors, problems, terminal output or changed files the same way.
• "Fix in Switchboard" in the quick fixes (⌘.) sends an error together with the lines it is on.
• It goes to the session you have open when it holds those files, or asks which one.
• Nothing goes to Claude until you press send in Switchboard.

It also works the other way round: when a session waits on you for a permission, a question or a plan, a VS Code notification lets you answer it without switching windows.

It's free on the VS Code Marketplace and Open VSX, and works in Cursor, Windsurf and VSCodium too.
